VOICE & VISION CLUB

Rules & Guidelines.

Our shared expectations help keep the club creative, respectful, and welcoming for everyone.

LEADERSHIP

Officers and their roles

Officers manage club projects and smaller parts of the club. The Team Manager manages teams and active projects. The Policy Manager helps ensure policies and rules are upheld. Other officers help with basic club needs, supplies, and organization.

Officer elections and removal

The club will begin with temporary officers. Over time, group meetings will include officer elections. Chosen officers will be asked to sign a form outlining their roles and responsibilities.

If an officer must be removed, the form signed at the beginning of their role will be voided.

MEMBERSHIP

Rules and regulations

  1. All members will be kind to each other.
  2. No profanity is permitted at club activities or on club materials.
  3. Listen to officers when they need assistance.
  4. Meetings are not mandatory, but attending is highly recommended.
  5. If you miss a meeting, you will be informed about what happened at the next meeting, through the website, or through the group chat.
  6. Big project ideas must be agreed upon by the group. Personal projects in a small group do not require a club vote, but everyone involved must agree.
  7. Projects that members want to start must be approved by an officer or the club leader.
  8. Members may email the leader with proposed club modifications, though requests may not always be approved.
  9. Tell an officer or the leader if you need help getting supplies so the club can assist.
  10. Everything created is independent of TWHS. Do not put anything related to TWHS on club projects, forms, or papers.
  11. Refer to signed forms for the main rules.

RESPONSIBILITY

Membership duties

  1. Participate in club activities. You may choose which activities, but you must participate in some.
  2. Attend at least 80% of meetings if possible. Email the leader if you cannot meet this expectation.
  3. Do not destroy or modify another person's project or work.
  4. Respect club property and TWHS property. These resources are not ours, so we should take care of them.
  5. Be respectful when giving feedback. Do not use name-calling, profanity, insults, or harmful comments. Feedback should help others improve.
  6. Refer to signed forms for the main rules.

Questions or concerns?

Speak with an officer or the club leader if you need clarification, support, or help following these guidelines.
